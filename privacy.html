隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-18 · 适用版本：1.0

支援 / SupportEnglish

长夜灯塔是一个本地优先的成长工具。我们不做用户画像，不投放广告，也没有自建服务器。这份政策只说明一件事：哪些数据留在你的设备上，哪些数据会在你主动操作时短暂离开设备。

1. 留在本机的数据

以下内容只写入本 App 在你这台设备上的存储，不会上传，我们无法读取：

  • 航标、泊位、时间安排与完成记录
  • 守灯计时记录、光程、灯油与星砂、灯塔阶位、属性
  • 航线、阶段里程碑、序章剧情进度、挑战进度
  • 同航者频道的动态、评论与点赞
  • 主题色、提醒开关、音乐开关、昵称与头像选择
  • 通过「设置 → 账号」绑定的本机身份信息（见第 2 节）

你可以随时在「设置 → 守灯人账号 → 删除账号与全部数据」清除以上全部内容。删除立即生效，且无法恢复。

2. Sign in with Apple

如果你选择使用 Apple 登录，本 App 会从 Apple 收到一个用户标识，以及你允许共享的昵称与邮箱。它们只保存在本机，用于在设置页显示绑定状态。我们没有后端服务，不会把这些信息上传到任何服务器，也不会用于追踪或广告。你可以在 App 内解除绑定，或在 iOS 设置中停止使用 Apple ID 登录本 App。

3. 定位与天气

天气是可选功能。只有当你主动打开或刷新天气时，本 App 才会请求一次使用期间的粗粒度位置，并把纬度与经度发送给 Open-Meteo（open-meteo.com）以获取当地天气。请求中不含姓名、账号标识或设备标识。

  • 本 App 不保存坐标，不做位置历史记录，也不在后台持续定位。
  • 你可以拒绝定位权限，App 的其他功能不受影响。
  • Open-Meteo 的数据处理方式见其官网的隐私说明。

4. 通知

守灯结束提醒使用 iOS 本地通知，在你设定的时间由系统在本机触发，不经过服务器。你可以随时在 App 内或 iOS 设置中关闭。

5. 我们不收集的数据

本 App 不包含广告 SDK、分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK，也不含第三方登录或内购。我们不会收集：精确位置、通讯录、照片、健康数据、剪贴板、IDFA、设备广告标识，或任何可用于跨 App 追踪的标识。

6. 同航者频道

频道页是写在本机的记事本，不是社交网络。你写下的动态与评论只保存在本机，不发起网络请求、不上传，也无法看到其他用户的内容。其他用户同样看不到你的内容。

7. 儿童

本 App 不面向儿童收集个人信息，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

8. 数据导出与备份

「设置 → 存档备份」可以把本机存档导出为文件，由你自己保存或转移。导出的文件不经过我们的服务器。

9. 政策更新

如果本 App 以后加入云同步、分享或其他会离开设备的功能，我们会先更新这份政策，并在 App 内说明，再发布对应版本。

10. 联系

如有隐私相关问题，请在项目仓库提交 issue。

ENGLISH

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18 · Applies to version 1.0

Nightbeacon is a local-first growth app. We do not profile users, run ads, or operate a server. This policy explains what stays on your device and what briefly leaves it when you ask for it.

1. Data stored on your device

The following is written only to Nightbeacon's storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and we cannot read it:

  • beacons, berths, schedules, and completion history
  • watch sessions, distance travelled, lantern oil, stardust, levels, and attributes
  • routes, milestones, prologue progress, and challenge progress
  • posts, comments, and likes in the channel tab
  • theme, reminder and music settings, display name, and avatar choice
  • the local identity created by Sign in with Apple (see section 2)

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Keeper account → Delete account and all data. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

2. Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a user identifier and the name and email address you agree to share. These are stored only on this device, to show the binding state in Settings. There is no backend, so nothing is uploaded, and the data is not used for tracking or advertising. You can unbind inside the app, or stop using Apple ID with Nightbeacon in iOS Settings.

3. Location and weather

Weather is optional. Only when you open or refresh it does the app request a single coarse location and send latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to fetch local weather. The request contains no name, account identifier, or device identifier.

  • The app does not store coordinates, keep location history, or track in the background.
  • If you deny location access, the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Open-Meteo's handling of data is described on its website.

4. Notifications

Watch reminders are local iOS notifications, scheduled and fired by the system on your device. They never pass through a server. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

5. What we do not collect

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, third-party login, or in-app purchase. We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, health data, clipboard contents, IDFA, advertising identifiers, or any identifier used for cross-app tracking.

6. The channel tab

The channel is a notebook kept on your device, not a social network. Posts and comments stay in local storage. The app makes no network request for them, uploads nothing, and cannot show other people's content. Other people cannot see yours.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from children, or from anyone.

8. Export and backup

Settings → Save backup exports your local save as a file you control. The file never passes through our servers.

9. Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, sharing, or anything else that leaves the device, we will update this policy and explain the change in the app before that version ships.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project repository.
